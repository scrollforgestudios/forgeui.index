ForgeGUI Campaign

Editor Workflow

Everything you need to turn provided ForgeGUI footage into a clean, native-feeling short.

Your job: take the footage you're given, repurpose it with simple edits, and submit it. You are not sourcing footage, creating from scratch, posting, or managing any account.

🎯 Overview

ForgeGUI is an AI tool for developers and creators — it generates thumbnails, 3D models, GUIs, skins, and even scripting/coding help. This campaign shows it off to Roblox and Fortnite developers through YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. The content must feel natural, clean, and organic — not like an ad.

🔁 The Editing Flow

1

Receive footage

You'll be given pre-made ForgeGUI clips — no sourcing or filming needed.

2

Repurpose it

Add captions, trim for pacing, apply simple zooms/cuts/transitions, and add music.

3

Keep it native

Simple UGC style, minimal, easy to understand — avoid anything that feels like aggressive promotion.

4

Show ForgeGUI clearly

The tool's features need to be visible — an actual demo moment is required, not just a mention.

5

Submit

Deliver the finished edit — submission details will be shared with you directly.

✂️ What Counts as "Repurposing"

💬Captions or text on screen
✂️Slight trimming or pacing changes
🔍Simple zooms, cuts, or transitions
🎵Music (recommended)

Never upload the provided footage with zero changes — it must feel repurposed, not reposted.

📤 How to Submit Your Edit

Once your edit is done, here's how to get it to us:

1

Upload to Google Drive

Upload your finished video file to Google Drive.

2

Set sharing to Public

Right-click the file → Share → under "General access," set it to "Anyone with the link" so it can be opened without permission requests.

3

Copy the link

Click "Copy link" once sharing is set.

4

Send it on Telegram

Paste the link and send it over @atlasdworks for review.
